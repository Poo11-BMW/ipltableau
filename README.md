
# 🏏 IPL Tableau Dashboard (2008–2020)

This project presents an interactive Tableau dashboard analyzing Indian Premier League (IPL) performance metrics across 13 seasons (2008–2020). The dashboard captures key aspects such as player accolades, match trends, and toss-based strategies to deliver valuable insights for analysts, fans, and cricket strategists.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

The IPL dashboard is designed to deliver season-wise insights with a high degree of interactivity and visual appeal:

- **🏆 Season Winner**: Displays the winning team for each IPL season from 2008 to 2020.
- **🎯 Orange Cap**: Highlights the top run-scorer of each season (e.g., Virat Kohli with 5,878 runs).
- **🎯 Purple Cap**: Identifies the highest wicket-taker (e.g., Lasith Malinga with 170 wickets).
- **🔥 Total Fours and Sixes**: Shows the cumulative number of fours and sixes hit each season.
- **⚖️ Matches Won Based on Toss Decision**: A horizontal bar chart detailing how teams performed based on their toss choices.
- **📈 Toss Decision-Based Win Percentage**: A donut chart comparing the success rate of choosing to bat versus field first.
- **📅 Interactive Filters**: Season filter (2008–2020) that updates all KPIs and visualizations dynamically for selected years.

## 🎯 Key Insights (2008–2020)

- Teams opting to **field first** after winning the toss have shown a higher win percentage (~60.78%).
- **Mumbai Indians** are the most consistent season winners.
- **Virat Kohli** (Orange Cap) and **Lasith Malinga** (Purple Cap) have dominated the leaderboard historically.
- The number of boundaries and sixes highlights the increasing aggressiveness of modern IPL gameplay.
- Season-specific trends can be uncovered by interacting with year filters.
